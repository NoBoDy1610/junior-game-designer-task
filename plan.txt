ROOM RENOVATION PLAN

1. Assess the room
Measure the space, list problems and decide what should change. Set a budget with a 10% reserve.

2. Prepare the work
Choose colors and materials. Compare prices, buy supplies and check that all tools are available.

3. Clear and protect
Move furniture, remove decorations and cover the floor, sockets and items that must stay.

4. Repair surfaces
Fill holes, sand uneven areas and remove dust. Apply primer where needed.

5. Paint
Paint the ceiling first, then the walls. Let the first coat dry before applying the second.

6. Restore the room
Remove protective covers, clean up, move furniture back and arrange lighting and decorations.

7. Inspect and finish
Check the paint and furniture placement, correct small defects, review costs and dispose of waste properly.
